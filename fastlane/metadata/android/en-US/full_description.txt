ProjectM TV is a music visualizer for Android TV. It runs projectM 4.1.7, the open-source reimplementation of Winamp's MilkDrop, with 9,606 presets from Jason Fletcher's "Cream of the Crop" collection.

It visualizes the music another app plays on the TV, such as SoundCloud. It is not a music player itself: start your music first, then open ProjectM TV.

<b>What it does</b>
<ul>
<li>Shows 9,606 presets in shuffled order, changing every 30 seconds by default or with Left/Right on the remote, with smooth blends between them</li>
<li>Shows the title and artist of each new track for 20 seconds (needs notification access, under Apps › Special app access in the TV's settings)</li>
<li>Adapts the render resolution to hold the frame rate, and caps it by the TV's memory so the music app is not closed</li>
<li>Finds the audio of the playing app about 1 second after launch, even where Android's standard visualizer hears nothing (e.g. NVIDIA SHIELD with Dolby output)</li>
<li>No freezes at preset switches: upcoming presets are prepared in the background</li>
<li>Replaces presets that stay black while music plays</li>
<li>Settings panel with transition style, detail, frame rate, audio source and live diagnostics</li>
</ul>

<b>Limits</b>
<ul>
<li>Validated on the NVIDIA SHIELD TV (2 GB) and SHIELD TV Pro (3 GB); other TVs should work but are untested. Not recommended for devices with 1 GB of memory</li>
<li>Needs Android 5.0+ with OpenGL ES 3.0 and the permission to record audio (to receive the music, not the microphone)</li>
<li>Only SoundCloud and Flow have been tested as music apps</li>
<li>Apps that block audio capture cannot be visualized</li>
</ul>
