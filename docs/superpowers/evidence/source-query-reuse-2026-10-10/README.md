# Source-query reuse and feedback memo lifetime

Angle/radius presence and sampling-coordinate uniformity reuse successful
answers within one analysis. Original predicates and short-circuit witnesses
remain unchanged. Uniformity keys include the complete basis tuple. Keys retain
their original node; a failed walk stores no partial answer. First queries retain
the existing traversal charge, and no configured limit increases.

The pre-lifetime-fix replay of the same frozen 2,000 selection recovers structured
reports for Grind 230 and DeepDiver. It has no lost structured descriptions;
1,997 of the previously structured descriptions have identical substantive
analysis. One historical feedback-envelope record differs. Twelve direct repeat
exports with the old and cache-only models both produce its bounded result;
this is an observed historical inconsistency, not proof of better fidelity.

A separate deterministic weak-reference regression exposed an existing local
feedback-envelope cache lifetime defect. Nested swizzles manufacture canonical
nodes; local memo tables kept their IDs while allowing those nodes to be released.
Later objects could reuse an ID. The regression fails before repair at the second
canonical lane and passes after retaining `(original_node, answer)` in local
affine, image-dependency and scalar-projection tables. The envelope table already
retains its node. The patch changes no formulas, presets, AAR or numeric policies.

The initial repaired controls pass 122 tests. Independent review passes 75
feedback/sensitivity/Q/projection/query controls, including the exact frozen
pool profiles. Cache-only qualification passes 3,119 tests plus 92 subtests;
its fixed 100 controls retain structured/schema-valid output. Final repaired
qualification is recorded separately in `checkpoint.json` when complete.

The repaired full suite passes 3,120 tests plus 92 subtests in 199.43 seconds.
Two later numerical controls pass separately with the 20-test feedback-envelope
file: four independent main samples retain all four coefficient matrices, and an
external-noise offset retains an unknown affine-main envelope.

The final compact replay finishes all 2,000 cases in 260.75 seconds. All 2,000
reports are structured; no earlier structured report is lost. Of the 1,998
previously structured reports, 1,963 retain identical substantive analysis and
35 change only `visual_description.feedback_envelope`. All 37 changed/new
descriptions validate the schema; unchanged descriptions inherit the prior
verified schema result. Sample-site identities, matrices and corrected unknowns
are retained in the compressed comparison. Native numeric/visual fidelity is
not claimed from this replay.

For the historical painterly case, twelve repaired repeats consistently retain
unknown: the authored warp includes `GetBlur1`, which this main-only affine
helper does not support. Its former bound therefore cannot be credited as a
successful prediction. `salvia with chip harris - culminatorics.milk` contains
four explicit main texture samples and now retains all four, while the historical
record retained three. The supplied numerical control verifies their independent
0.25 coefficients.

## Coverage and unresolved calculations

A structured report is not complete interpretation. The pre-lifetime-fix replay
retains per-field budget reasons in 109 presets, including flashing scalar
projection in 84. DeepDiver still reports locally caught traversal-budget
unknowns. The summary's top-level `budget_exhausted` flag currently covers
uncaught phase/description failures; it does not reliably summarize every
locally caught failure. Do not infer zero gaps from that flag or 2,000 structured
records. The next reporting repair must preserve those reasons and make that
distinction explicit.
